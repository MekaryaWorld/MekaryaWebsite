How a kit is made

Digitally engineered, hand-finished. Every kit goes through the same four steps.

  1. Scan

    We scan the car first, so every part is designed on screen to its real body lines, not a catalogue's.

  2. Shape

    The design is printed, then laid up and shaped by hand in fiberglass, hybrid carbon or full carbon.

  3. Fit

    Raw parts are test-fitted on your car before anything is painted.

  4. Finish

    Then they're finished, fitted and handed back. This Impreza wears the Perisai full panel.
